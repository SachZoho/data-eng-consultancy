## The Challenge
HealthData AI had data silos across five different regional offices. Joining these datasets for a global view required manual exports and risky manual merges.

## Our Approach
We leveraged **Unity Catalog** for centralized governance. By implementing a shared metadata layer, we allowed data scientists to discover and query datasets across regions securely without moving the data unnecessarily.

## The Outcome
The new infrastructure allowed the team to iterate on their lapped-lung cancer detection model 3x faster, leading to a breakthrough in early detection rates.
