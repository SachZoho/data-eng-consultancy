## The Challenge
Fintech Corp had grown rapidly, and their legacy data pipelines couldn't keep up. Data scientists were spending 40% of their time cleaning data rather than analyzing it.

## Our Approach
We didn't just replace the tools; we redesigned the flow. We implemented a **Bronze-Silver-Gold** architecture:
1. **Bronze**: Raw ingestion for auditability.
2. **Silver**: Cleaned, deduplicated, and validated data.
3. **Gold**: Business-ready aggregates for reporting.

## The Outcome
The transition enabled the company to launch a new real-time fraud detection system that saved them an estimated $1.2M in the first quarter alone.
